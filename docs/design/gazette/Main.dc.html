C · Signature motion

The stamp and the strike

As a record scrolls into view its status types itself from PROPOSED to an ACCEPTED stamp, then each rejected alternative is struck through, left to right. Motion shows that a choice was made, and what lost.

Reduced motion: records render in their final state. Stamp and strike are real text, so screen readers hear “rejected” rather than a style.

Dark theme: ink paper #16140F, stamp #F08A6C. 360px: metadata column folds above the headline as a single mono line.
